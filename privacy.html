Privacy policy

MindfulFast · last updated 7 August 2026

MindfulFast does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. Everything you enter stays in your browser storage on your own device.

What the app stores

All of the following is kept locally, in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB, on your device only:

  • Your chosen fasting protocol, and the start and end times of fasts
  • Alarm preferences and scheduled alarm times
  • Regular eating times and which slots you have marked as eaten
  • Urge episodes: time, situation, location, mood, urge and control ratings, what you tried, the outcome, and any notes or setback review you write
  • Weight entries you choose to log

None of this is sent anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting service in the app.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage from your browser or device settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes everything. There is no copy elsewhere for us to delete, and no way for us to recover it for you.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them for fasting alarms and meal reminders. Permission is asked for only when you enable an alarm, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings. Alarm times are held on your device.

The optional push server

A page cannot reliably run a timer once it is closed, so exact alarm delivery needs a small server to send a push at the right moment. This is off by default. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to connect one and enter its address yourself.

If you do connect one, it receives only:

  • Your browser's push subscription, which is an anonymous endpoint issued by your browser vendor's push service
  • The times of your upcoming alarms and their notification text, for example "Eating window open"

It never receives your weight, urge log, fasting history, moods, or notes. Alarm records are deleted once delivered, and subscriptions are dropped when the push service reports them as expired. If you run the server yourself, which is the intended use, you are the only person with access to it. Disconnecting it in the app unsubscribes and asks the server to forget your device.

Fonts and offline use

The app ships its own copy of the IBM Plex typefaces and caches its files for offline use with a service worker. It does not load anything from a content delivery network or third-party host, so no third party sees a request from you.

Children

MindfulFast is not intended for anyone under 18 and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Health information

The app is a self-help tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Cortisol figures are an estimate produced from a published population model of the daily rhythm, calculated from the time of day. Nothing is measured from your body, and the estimate is not a clinical reading.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the current version is always the one published with the app.

Contact
