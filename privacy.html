Privacy policy

MindfulFast - last updated 10 August 2026

MindfulFast stores data only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your information. There is no MindfulFast cloud account and no remote copy of your log.

Local-only storage

Fasting times, sleep and wake logs, alarms, urge notes, weight entries, and settings stay in on-device storage (localStorage / IndexedDB). Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes them. We cannot recover that data for you.

No data collection or transmission

  • No analytics
  • No ads
  • No crash or diagnostics SDKs
  • No cloud sync
  • No accounts or sign-in

Ordinary use does not send your health log or settings to us or to third parties. The app is built to work offline.

An optional push helper for exact alarms while the app is closed is off by default. Nothing is sent unless you paste a server address yourself. That path is not part of normal use.

Wellness disclaimer

MindfulFast is a wellness and self-help tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace care from a clinician. Cortisol figures are a population-model estimate from your wake time on this device, not a body measurement or clinical reading.

Fasting is not right for everyone. Speak with a doctor before use if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, diabetic, take medication with food, or have a history of disordered eating. If eating feels out of control, get help from a qualified professional.
