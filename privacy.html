Privacy policy

MindfulFast - last updated 10 August 2026

We do not collect your data. MindfulFast is strictly local. Everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no MindfulFast server receiving it.

What this means

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services
  • No cloud sync and no remote backup by us
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because we never receive it

The app is designed to work offline. Your fasting timer, urge log, weight entries, and settings live in storage on this phone or browser only.

What the app stores on your device

The following may be kept locally in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB:

  • Your chosen fasting protocol, and the start and end times of fasts
  • Alarm preferences and scheduled alarm times
  • Regular eating times and which slots you have marked as eaten
  • Urge episodes: time, situation, location, mood, urge and control ratings, what you tried, the outcome, and any notes or setback review you write
  • Weight entries you choose to log
  • Your wake time, used only to shift the cortisol estimate on this device

That data does not leave the device as part of normal use.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage in your browser or device settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes everything stored locally. There is no copy on our side to erase or restore.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them for fasting alarms and meal reminders. Permission is asked only when you enable an alarm, and you can turn it off in your device settings. Alarm times are held on your device.

Optional push server (off by default)

A web page cannot reliably fire an alarm after it is fully closed. Exact delivery while the app is closed needs a small push helper. That helper is off by default. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to connect one and paste its address yourself.

If you connect one, it receives only:

  • Your browser's push subscription (an anonymous endpoint from the browser vendor's push service)
  • Upcoming alarm times and short notification text, for example “Eating window open”

It never receives your weight, urge log, fasting history, moods, or notes. If you run the helper yourself, which is the intended use, you control that machine. Disconnecting it in the app unsubscribes and asks it to forget this device.

Unless you turn that option on, MindfulFast stays fully local.

Fonts and offline files

The app ships its own Figtree and Literata font files and can cache its pages with a service worker for offline use. It does not load fonts or scripts from a third-party CDN, so ordinary use does not phone home to other companies for those assets.

Children

MindfulFast is not intended for anyone under 18 and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because the default product does not collect information at all.

Health information

The app is a self-help tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Cortisol figures are an estimate from a published population model of the daily rhythm, calculated from your wake time on this device. Nothing is measured from your body, and the estimate is not a clinical reading.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The version published with the app is the current one.

Contact
