Why this matters

Chronically elevated cortisol is associated with visceral fat storage, higher ghrelin, and reduced insulin sensitivity. Aligning your eating window with the natural cortisol decline may help, though the effect varies between people and this is a general pattern rather than a measurement of you.

Before you start

MindfulFast is a self-help tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace a clinician.

The cortisol readings are an estimate from a population model of the daily rhythm, calculated from the time of day. Nothing is measured from your body.

Fasting does not suit everyone. Talk to a doctor first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, diabetic, taking medication with food, or have a history of disordered eating.

If eating feels out of control, or thoughts about food and weight are taking over, that is worth telling someone. A clinician trained in CBT-E can help more than any app.

Everything you log stays on this device. No account, no servers, nothing collected.
