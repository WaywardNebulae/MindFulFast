    Privacy

    No account, no analytics, no ads, no crash tools that send reports off your phone, and no cloud sync. Your log stays on your phone. If you uninstall or clear the app storage, it is gone unless you exported a backup first. See the privacy policy.

    Wellness

    MindfulFast is a self-help wellness tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Timing estimates are based on what you log. They do not measure your body.

    Before you start

    Wellness. This needs to be said. MindfulFast is a self-help wellness tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace a doctor or therapist.

    Timing tips are a general estimate from a daily pattern, based on the wake time you log and what else you enter. We work from what you put in. We do not know your exact levels, and nothing is measured from your body. These are estimates, not clinical claims.

    Fasting is not for everyone. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, diabetic, take medication with food, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor first. We want you to be safe.

    If eating feels completely out of control, a CBT-E clinician can help more than any app can. Talk to your doctor or therapist when you need to. This app sits next to real medical care. It does not replace it.

    Privacy mattered a lot to us. As far as this build goes: no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash tools that send reports off your phone, no cloud sync. Your log stays on your device. If you uninstall or clear the app storage, it is gone and we cannot get it back. The privacy policy says the same thing, and we keep it matched to Google Play Data Safety if the build ever changes.
